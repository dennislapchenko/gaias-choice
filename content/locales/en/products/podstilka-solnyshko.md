1. **Verdict** — one or two sentences for the reader who leaves after ten
   seconds: would we buy the Solnyshko mat again, knowing what we know now?
2. **Why we bought it** — nobody wakes up wanting a review; they wake up with
   a problem. What were we tired of — damp grass, gravel, a baby with nowhere
   clean to be put down? What were we afraid of, and what were we choosing
   between: a blanket, a foam mat, nothing at all?
3. **How it lived with us** — how long, and on what ground: campsites, forest
   floor, roadside gravel, the camper floor? Bought or gifted (gifted must be
   said out loud)? How does it wash and dry on the road, what stained or
   frayed, what would we genuinely miss if it vanished tomorrow?
4. **What's genuinely good** — concrete beats adjectives: how small it packs,
   what it weighs, how many washes it has had, what it really cost. What's the
   one specific moment it earned its place?
5. **What we'd change** — every review names a flaw; if nothing else, the flaw
   is the price, or who it's *not* for. Size, fabric, the way it holds dirt or
   moisture — and the question a paid reviewer never answers: what would make
   us take this recommendation back?
6. **Who it's for** — and who should skip it, one honest line each. How long
   could we happily do without it — would a plain blanket do the same job?
