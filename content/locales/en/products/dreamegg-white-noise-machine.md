1. **Verdict** — one or two sentences for the reader who leaves after ten
   seconds: would we buy the Dreamegg again, knowing what we know now?
2. **Why we bought it** — nobody wakes up wanting a review; they wake up with
   a problem at 3 a.m. What was actually waking the baby — campsite noise,
   traffic, thin camper walls, us moving around a metre away? What were we
   afraid would go wrong, and what were we choosing between: a phone app, a
   fan, silence?
3. **How it lived with us** — how long, and in what conditions: which nights,
   which sounds, what volume, running on battery or plugged in? Bought or
   gifted (gifted must be said out loud)? What broke or wore out, and what
   would we genuinely miss if it vanished tomorrow?
4. **What's genuinely good** — concrete beats adjectives: how long the charge
   really lasts, which sounds we actually use, how it handles being packed and
   knocked about, what it cost. What's the one specific night it earned its
   place?
5. **What we'd change** — every review names a flaw; if nothing else, the flaw
   is the price, or who it's *not* for. Buttons, light, the sound loop, the
   habit it builds — and the question a paid reviewer never answers: what
   would make us take this recommendation back?
6. **Who it's for** — and who should skip it, one honest line each. How long
   could we happily do without it, and would we want the baby to sleep without
   it?
