1. **Verdict** — one or two sentences for the reader who leaves after ten
   seconds: would we buy the Picopresso again, knowing what pulling a shot
   with it actually takes?
2. **Why we bought it** — nobody wakes up wanting a review; they wake up with
   a problem at 3 a.m. What morning-coffee compromise on the road were we
   done accepting — instant, gas-station espresso, a heavy machine we
   couldn't pack — and what pushed us toward a hand-pumped one instead?
3. **How it lived with us** — how many trips, in what setup (van, tent,
   hotel room), and with what water and grinder alongside it? Bought or
   gifted (gifted must be said out loud)? What part wore out or needed
   cleaning, and what would we miss most if we left it behind next time?
4. **What's genuinely good** — concrete beats adjectives: how many pumps,
   how many grams of coffee, how many minutes from kettle to cup, what a
   real shot tasted like next to a café one? What's the one specific moment
   it earned its place in the bag?
5. **What we'd change** — every review names a flaw; if nothing else, the
   flaw is the arm workout, the mess, or who it's *not* for. And the
   question a paid reviewer never answers: what would make us leave the
   Picopresso at home next trip?
6. **Who it's for** — and who should just pack instant coffee instead, one
   honest line each. How long could we happily travel without it?
